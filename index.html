I want to show y’all something I brought with me this evening, But before I hold up this vial I just want to tell you why this vial is so special to me, See my life began with a miracle that almost ended in tragedy. In May 1981, at Cox North, both my mother and I were hovering on the very edge of death. See she had had her tubes "tied" and with that confidence lead my arrival. I had somehow made it past the "tie" and now we both had a problem! It was the first time God showed me He had a plan for me, even when the lights were flickering low.

Growing up, I lived between two completely different worlds. On one side, there was my mother—a wild spirit, surrounded by the grit and darkness of the 70-80's biker life. Running Grant Street in the early 80's till the street lights came on, oh what a time. On the other, there was my Nana and Papa, My Nana was a godly woman whose home felt like a sanctuary of peace. She was the "glue" that brought all my family and cuz'ins togther on a Sunday afternoon, I saw both sides of light and dark: the chaos that wanted to consume me, and the grace that tried to sustain me.

But for a long time, the darkness won. I always had the allure of not being a milktoast man, and seen the effects of fear and intimadation. It was when I turned 13yo, after a sudden airborne illness, called Transversemitolitous, left me paralyzed from the waist down for 7 days, My brother Raymond of 19yo died within days, but God...... He got me past my first knot, and through the Power of Prayer I was walking in 7 days but then in 9th grade my left foot deformed sideways for unknown reasons. Entering High School as a cripple was to much and I lost my way. I started drinking very heavly and time flew by to 2006. I became like those glow-in-the-dark stars you see on children's ceilings—only 'bright' when I was chasing the next bottle or the next party, but completely dark and empty the moment the external lights went out. I was living entirely on external stimulation: alcohol, drugs, and a lifestyle that was rapidly extinguishing my soul.

Then came the collision.

You see, tritium isn’t just found; it is forged through a violent impact, much like a supernova. On March 14th, 2006, my life experienced its own supernova. In an act of violence that changed everything, I shot a man. Once again, we arrive at Cox North—the same place where my life began—a life was hanging in the balance. A man was brought in, considered DOA, with a softball sized hole in his shoulder that looked like it would be his end. And I was facing a lifetime behind bars.

In that moment of absolute darkness, there were no 'party lights' left to charge me. There was only the raw, terrifying reality of what I had done.

But it was in the middle of that wreckage—in a Jail cell, Looking down on David Stidman leading a group a "walking dead men" Singing and praying in the B Pod gym — that is when I discovered the secret of this vial. I realized that I didn't need an environment to make me bright; I needed a Savior to change my very nature. God reached into the mire of my life and placed His Spirit inside of me. He changed my identity, much like a neutron strike changes hydrogen into tritium.

Today, I don’t shine because the world is bright; I shine because of what resides within. I am no longer that flickering star, dependent on the world to keep me glowing. Through Christ, I have become a steady, enduring light—unbothered by the darkness, because my power source is no longer external. It is internal.

But God uses collisions to create something new. My 'supernova' moment happened in the middle of a tragedy—a shooting, an arrest, and a prison sentence that threatened to break me forever. It was in the darkest, most pressurized moment of my life—in a jail pod, surrounded by darkness—that the collision happened. I stopped looking for external light and asked Christ to put His Spirit inside of me. I redicated my life to Christ there, getting baptized inside GCJ infront of some of the most dangerious men in town at the time. I went from a man who was fading away to a man who carries a steady, internal flame that no prison cell or hardship can blow out. EVER!!

Now to the Message!

Unless you’re sitting close here, you probably can’t see exactly what I’m holding, so brought a few vials to show during this message so feel free to pick one up and check it out. It’s just a little piece of 3D printed filament on a ball chain with a tiny glass tube inside. But there’s something mighty special about this little tube. Inside this glass is a gas called tritium (H3).

Now, I’m no physicist, and as much as I want to bore you with a science lesson, I won't, but I need to explain how this works. Tritium isn't just found; it is forged. It requires a moment of cosmic impact. Picture a supernova—a star exploding with such ferocity that it breaks the very fabric of its own composition. In the heat of that explosion, neutrons are liberated, flying through space like tiny bullets. When one of these high-speed neutrons strikes a standard atom of Hydrogen, it changes that atom's identity forever, remember that. It adds weight, it adds complexity, and it creates Tritium. It is an element born of collision.

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." — 2 Corinthians 5:17

Tritium is a special radioactive isotope that is constantly giving off energy. The inside of this little glass tube is coated with a green powder. When the energy from the Tritium hits that green powder, it glows green. So am I the empty glass tube coated with green gunk?

( Passing the vial around — Adults only please )

In fact, if we were to turn off every single light right now, and board up the windows, you would see this little vial glowing a bright, steady green. Keep that word STEADY in your frontal lobe. We’ve all seen glow-in-the-dark stuff before, right? When I bought my home over 13 years ago, it had those cheap glow in the dark stars on the living room ceiling... today they dream of shining, but have lost the ability long ago. I still left them up there to this day!. Those plastic stars have to be charged up by the lights in the room. Their light is entirely dependent on the environment around them. (Remind you of anyone you know?)

But not tritium. It doesn't need external light to charge it up. It will glow steadily, without fading, for over two decades, just off the energy it holds within. The source of its power is on the INSIDE.

The Scripture

Brothers and sisters, when I learned how this little vial worked, I couldn’t help but think about what Jesus said to us on the side of that mountain:

"You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden. Nor do people light a lamp and put it under a basket, but on a stand, and it gives light to all in the house. In the same way, let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven." — Matthew 5:14-16

I want to offer you three simple truths today about what it means to be a "Tritium Christian" in a dark world.

Point Number One

Our power source is internal, not external.

There are a lot of folks walking around today who are like those plastic glow-in-the-dark stars. They look bright on Sunday morning when they’re in the sanctuary, surrounded by the warm light of the church. But then Monday morning rolls around. They go out into a dark world—into the workplace, into the schoolhouse, into a culture that does not love the Lord.

"But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth." — Act 1:8

Because they are operating on borrowed light, their glow doesn't last. By Tuesday, they are blending right back into the darkness around them. They compromise, they lose their joy, and they hide their faith because they’ve been disconnected from their external charging station.

Their light source is only external, they fade out. But a born-again, blood-bought Christian is like this tritium vial. When you give your life to Jesus Christ, He doesn't just give you a new set of rules to follow. He puts His very Spirit INSIDE OF YOU!

"If the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Spirit who dwells in you." — Romans 8:11

You dont need the culture to agree with you or to even validate YOU to keep shining. Stop rationalizing and justifying reasons to not shine

Point Number Two

The darker the room, the brighter the light.

If I take this tritium vial outside at high noon in the middle of a Missouri summer, you won't even notice it's glowing. But if I take it down into a storm cellar in the middle of the night, it shines like a beacon.

Church, we look around at the news today, and worry and plan. Some people say "Joel, the world is getting so dark. Things are getting so bad." And I’m not going to argue with you; there is a lot of darkness out there. But Philippians 2:15 tells us

"that you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and twisted generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world," — Philippians 2:15

Revelation chapter 6 describes the actual manifestation of these things through the opening of the Seals: The Red Horse (War), the Black Horse (Famine), and the Pale Horse (Death/Disease) represent the ultimate. While these seals show a world being broken and bent toward destruction, they are part of a controlled sequence. Even in the chaos of the seals, God is still the one "opening" them—He remains the Master of the process

God did not call us to complain about the darkness. He called us to shine in it! The darkness isn't a threat to the tritium; the darkness is the tritium's opportunity to do exactly what it was made to do. Shine Bright!

Point Number Three

Remember that word I asked you to keep in your fontal lobe?

Webster’s Dictionary – “Steady” Firm and solid; not moving or shaking. Constant, regular, unvarying; continuing without interruption. Showing firmness of purpose, resolve, or character. It’s a steady, lasting light. Steadiness reflects perseverance. James 1:12 says,

"Blessed is the man who remains steadfast under trial, for when he has stood the test he will receive vthe crown of life, wwhich God has promised to those who love him." — James 1:12

This little vial doesn’t flash like a strobe light. It doesn’t explode like a firecracker. It just gives off a quiet, steady, enduring glow, day after day, year after year.

Sometimes, in our walk with Christ, we think we need to be a fireworks show. But let me tell you what changes the world: a Christian who just keeps glowing like Frank Childers. Year‑after‑year impact. When a light shines consistently over many years, it becomes a landmark for travelers, just like a Lighthouse by the ocean. A Christian who “keeps glowing” like Frank becomes a rliable beacon in a dark world. It makes me think of my Brother David Stidman, 20+ years in maximum security prison, talk about dark—and the Holy Spirit keeps him glowing.

A Christian who is faithful to their spouse, year after year. A parent who faithfully prays for their wayward child, night after night. A church member who shows up and serves, week after week. It’s that quiet, steady, internal light that points a lost world to Jesus.
